# Showcase direction

Review date: November 9, 2026. This is a proposed creative revision, not a new
render or an approved script. The existing 88-second delivery remains unchanged.

## What the reference does better

The reference is the 66-second [Website Cloner film](https://kujolang.ai/assets/videos/website-cloner-v0.1.1.mp4).
Its decoded frame sheet, captions, production notes and mix script were inspected.
Audio was not auditioned; comments about musical intent come from its generation
receipt and arrangement, not a listening assessment.

The film makes the benefit visible: the original site and its clone scroll beside
each other. That comparison occupies about 22 seconds. Narration stops for nearly
nine seconds while viewers examine the result. Six voice cues leave room for the
product and music. The original instrumental brief calls for a 108 BPM electronic
groove, warm bass, dry percussion and a lift after twenty seconds.

Hermes uses about 77 seconds of speech in an 88-second film. Eight body scenes
follow a similar pattern: headline, terminal, explanation, glitch. The footage is
authentic, but the rhythm makes each tool feel like another item to explain.
Installation interrupts the checkout story before its failure and repair develop.
More glitches or louder music alone would leave that structure intact.

## The change that matters

Make the corrected checkout the main event. Let viewers follow the failure,
Hermes's edit and the same tests passing. Leave a short pause on the passing result
so they can read it. Explain the benefit of retained evidence after that payoff.
Move installation near the end, when viewers have a reason to want the plugin.

Aim for 65–72 seconds with roughly 110–130 spoken words. Those are editing targets,
not measured delivery claims. Build the cuts around a restrained, upbeat music bed;
duck it under speech and let it rise during the result. Keep Siren, with shorter,
more conversational takes. Audition the mix before accepting it.

## Keep the proof and reduce the clutter

- Preserve square framing, complete screen edges, existing bumpers and CMD glitches
  at scene changes and both bumper joins. Use transitions selectively on musical beats.
- Keep one headline per shot. Highlight the actual failing value and passing count
  without covering or cropping the underlying screen. Do not invent a dashboard.
- Reuse the verified free-model session and its source timestamps. Retain the
  time-compression disclosure, approval boundaries and unchanged-test evidence.
- Passing these three checks proves the demonstrated cases, not general checkout
  correctness. State that scope in a short caption or accompanying description;
  do not claim a release gate or comprehensive verification.
- Match every spoken capability to the visible action. Catalog discovery must not
  imply that ShipCheck, Scout or PackWrite ran during the checkout session.
- Do not copy the reference's customer captures into this repository. Borrow its
  pacing and room for a visible result, not its private assets.
- Render a 480-square download after the revised edit passes source-frame checks,
  full decode, phone-size inspection and an actual listening pass.

The intended memorable moment is specific: the checkout overcharges a customer,
Hermes fixes the cause, and the unchanged checks pass with evidence still available.
That result should carry the excitement.
